## 🔍 Domain Selection Tips

### ✅ Good Domain Names:
- Short and memorable (goldmart ✅)
- Easy to spell
- Related to your business
- .com extension (most trusted)
- No hyphens or numbers

### ❌ Avoid:
- Too long or complicated
- Hard to spell
- Hyphens or numbers
- Unrelated extensions
- Trademark conflicts
